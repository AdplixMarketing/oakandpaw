Privacy Policy

Your trust matters to us. Here is how we handle the information you share.

  • Home
  • Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oak & Paw Pet Services, LLC (“Oak & Paw,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website or use our dog walking, pet sitting, and boarding services in the Chicago suburbs.

Information we collect

We collect only the information we need to respond to you and care for your pet.

Information you give us

  • Contact and booking details you submit through our contact form, including your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, information about your pet, and any message you send.
  • Communications when you call, text, email, or message us on social media.
  • Service information needed to provide care, such as your address, access instructions, veterinary contact, feeding and medication routines, and your pet’s health and behavior notes.

Information collected automatically

  • Our website is hosted on Vercel, which may log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device, and the pages you visit, for security and reliability.
  • We load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google may receive your IP address when those fonts are served.
  • We do not use invasive advertising trackers on this site.

How we use your information

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule a meet & greet.
  • Provide, manage, and confirm walking, sitting, and boarding services.
  • Send you updates about your pet, including photos and check-ins during visits.
  • Handle scheduling, invoicing, and payment for services you request.
  • Keep our website secure and improve how it works.
  • Comply with our legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as needed:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as our form delivery service (FormSubmit) and our website host (Vercel). These providers process information on our behalf.
  • Legal reasons, if required to respond to a lawful request, protect our rights, or address safety concerns.
  • A business transfer, if Oak & Paw is involved in a merger, sale, or transfer of assets.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses minimal cookies. Any cookies that are set are used to make the site function properly. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; the site will still work without them.

How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain our records, and meet legal, tax, or accounting requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or remove identifying details. You may ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Ask us to correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your information, subject to any records we must legally keep.
  • Opt out of non-essential messages at any time by telling us.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below.

Children’s privacy

Our services and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will remove it.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep your information safe.

Links to other sites

Our website may link to third-party sites, such as our Instagram and Facebook pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes will be reflected on this page.
