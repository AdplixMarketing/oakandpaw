01 — Dog Walking

Daily walks your dog will look forward to.

Consistent timing, steady pacing, and proper leash manners. We walk one dog at a time (or pets from the same home), so your pup gets undivided attention every step of the way.

  • 30 or 60‑minute solo walks tailored to energy level and weather
  • Fresh water, paw checks, and quick refresh on cues during every walk
  • GPS‑tracked routes and a photo update after each visit
  • Lockbox or key handling with full chain‑of‑custody
  • Backup walker covered by the same insurance — never a missed visit

02 — Pet Sitting

Home visits while you’re away.

Your pet stays comfortable in their own space while we handle feeding, fresh water, medications, playtime, and a quick tidy up of any accidents.

  • 30‑minute drop‑in visits, multiple times per day
  • Feeding, water refill, and medication administration (oral & topical)
  • Crate care, plant watering, and mail pickup on request
  • Dogs of all sizes, ages, and temperaments welcome
  • Detailed text report after every visit, with photos

03 — Boarding

Overnight stays in a calm, pet‑first home.

A small, fully dog‑proofed home — never a kennel. Just one guest at a time, so your dog has my full attention with structured walks, play, and plenty of rest.

  • Meet & greet required before first stay to ensure a good fit
  • Two leashed walks per day plus enrichment and play sessions
  • Familiar bedding and toys from home always welcome
  • Updates every evening with photos and a short note about the day
  • Pickup and drop‑off available in nearby suburbs for an added fee
