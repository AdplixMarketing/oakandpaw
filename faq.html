Common questions

What areas of Chicago do you serve?

We serve Lincoln Park, Lakeview, West Loop, River North, Logan Square, Wicker Park, Bucktown, Old Town, and surrounding neighborhoods. Outside that range, contact us and we’ll let you know what’s possible.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. Oak & Paw is fully insured and bonded on every walk, sitting visit, and overnight stay. Proof of coverage is available on request.

Do you require a meet & greet before booking?

Yes, for new clients. The meet and greet is free and usually takes about 20 minutes. It lets your pet get comfortable with us and gives us a chance to learn their routine, preferences, and any quirks we should know about.

How do you handle keys and home access?

We use a labeled, secure key system or a lockbox/smart‑lock code, whichever you prefer. Keys are never labeled with your address, and we keep an internal chain‑of‑custody log for every key.

What if my dog is reactive, shy, or anxious?

We have extensive experience with reactive and anxious dogs thanks to our trainer background. Tell us about your dog’s history during the meet and greet and we’ll design a plan that keeps them safe and comfortable — including quieter walk routes, gentle handling, and clear cues.

Will I get updates while you’re visiting?

Always. You’ll receive a text and a photo after every walk or visit, plus a longer recap at the end of each day for sittings and boarding stays.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made at least 24 hours before a scheduled visit are free. Same‑day cancellations may be charged 50% to cover the reserved time. Holiday bookings have a 7‑day cancellation window because demand is high.

Do you administer medication?

Yes — oral, topical, and ear/eye medications at no extra charge. Injections are case‑by‑case; let us know what your pet needs and we’ll confirm before booking.

What vaccinations do you require?

For dogs: rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella up to date. For cats: rabies and FVRCP. We may ask for proof before the first visit.

How do I pay, and when?

Invoices are sent at the end of the week for recurring services or after each booking for sittings and boarding. We accept Zelle, Venmo, credit/debit cards, and ACH.

Do you walk multiple dogs at once?

Only dogs from the same household. We don’t do shared group walks — every dog gets focused, one‑on‑one attention.

Can you help with basic training during visits?

Absolutely. As a certified obedience trainer, we can reinforce sit, stay, leash manners, and recall during regular walks. Dedicated training sessions are also available — ask for details.

Still have questions?

We’re happy to help.
