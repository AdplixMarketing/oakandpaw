Common questions

What areas do you serve?

We serve Oak Park, Oak Lawn, Oak Brook, and the surrounding Chicago suburbs. Outside that range, contact us and we’ll let you know what’s possible.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. Oak & Paw is fully insured and bonded on every walk, sitting visit, and overnight stay. Proof of coverage is available on request.

Do you require a meet & greet before booking?

Yes, for new clients. The meet and greet is free and usually takes about 15 minutes. It lets your dog get comfortable with us and gives us a chance to learn their routine, preferences, and any quirks we should know about.

How do you handle keys and home access?

We use a labeled, secure key system or a lockbox/smart‑lock code, whichever you prefer. Keys are never labeled with your address, and we keep an internal chain‑of‑custody log for every key.

What if my dog is reactive, shy, or anxious?

We have extensive experience with reactive and anxious dogs thanks to our trainer background. Tell us about your dog’s history during the meet and greet and we’ll design a plan that keeps them safe and comfortable — including quieter walk routes, gentle handling, and clear cues.

Will I get updates while you’re visiting?

Always. You’ll receive a text and a photo after every walk or visit, plus a longer recap at the end of each day for sittings and boarding stays.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made at least 24 hours before a scheduled visit are free. Same‑day cancellations are charged the full visit rate to cover the reserved time. Holiday bookings have a 7‑day cancellation window because demand is high.

Do you administer medication?

Yes — oral pills/liquid and topical (ointments, ear and eye drops) at no extra charge. Subcutaneous injections (e.g. insulin) aren’t offered at this time.

What vaccinations do you require?

Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella up to date. We may ask for proof before the first visit.

How do I pay, and when?

Invoices are sent after each visit. We accept Zelle, Cash App, and cash.

Do you walk multiple dogs at once?

Only dogs from the same household. We don’t do shared group walks — every dog gets focused, one‑on‑one attention.

Can you help with basic training during visits?

Yes. As a certified obedience trainer, Alexa reinforces sit, stay, leash manners, and recall during regular walks at no extra charge. Dedicated standalone training sessions aren’t offered at the moment — feel free to ask about availability.

Still have questions?
