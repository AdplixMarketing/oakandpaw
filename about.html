Who we are

A trainer’s eye, a sitter’s heart.

Oak & Paw was founded by Alexa Rios Torres — a certified dog obedience trainer (Ashworth College, 2015) with more than a decade of hands‑on experience caring for dogs of every temperament.

What started as helping friends and family quickly became an intentional, owner‑operated business serving the Chicago suburbs. Every visit reflects training principles, not just routine. Alexa notices the small things — a slight limp, a change in appetite, a new sound that worries your dog — and tells you right away.

And it’s only ever Alexa. No random strangers, no rotating walkers — just one trusted caretaker who builds a real bond with your dog over time. That’s the whole point.
