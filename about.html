Who we are

A trainer’s eye, a sitter’s heart.

Oak & Paw was founded by a certified dog obedience trainer who has spent more than a decade hands‑on with dogs and cats of every temperament — from anxious rescues to working breeds with serious energy to burn.

What started as helping friends and family quickly became a small, intentional business serving Chicago neighborhoods we know well. Every visit reflects training principles, not just routine. We notice the small things: a slight limp, a change in appetite, a new sound that worries your dog. Then we tell you.

We stay small on purpose. Limited spots means more time with each pet, a better understanding of their personality, and a level of trust you can feel from the very first meet & greet.
